How it works

  1. Pick a week. Each week has radicals, kanji, and vocabulary lessons.
  2. Study the radicals first — every kanji is built from them.
  3. Then study the kanji and the vocabulary that uses them.
  4. Take the three tests: radicals, kanji, vocabulary. Repeat any test whenever you want; nothing is locked.
